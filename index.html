The big sun is bright and warm in the blue sky above our head today. A little bird sings a happy song on the green tree near the old house. The soft wind blows through the open window and makes the white curtain move slowly. A small cat sleeps on the cozy rug near the warm fire in the room. The young boy walks to the school with his heavy bag full of new books to read. He sees a green frog jump into the clear water of the small clean pond. Many people are working hard in the large city to make a good living for them.They drive fast cars or ride a bus to get to their office on time every single day. Each person has a different dream and a special story to share with the whole world. Some like to travel to far places across the deep blue sea on a big ship with friends. Others just want to stay at home and play fun games with their loving family members. It is very good to be kind and help other people who are sad or sick. A nice smile can make a dark day look bright and happy for anyone we meet.We can learn so many cool things from reading good books at the town library every week. Books can take us on great trips to magical lands full of fun and big adventure. The world is so huge and has so many cool secrets waiting for us to find. We should always ask good questions to know more about the nature around us every day. Nature gives us beautiful things like tall mountains deep valleys and clear rivers to see. In the spring time we see pretty flowers grow and the fields turn fresh and green again.In the hot summer the days are very long and we can swim in the cool lake water. Autumn brings pretty leaves of red yellow and gold that fall from the large old trees. Cold winter covers the hard ground with a soft white blanket of clean and cold snow. Every season is special and has its own magic for us to enjoy with our good friends. We can find true joy in the small things of our daily life like a good hot drink. Drinking hot tea on a cold day makes us feel so warm and happy inside our home.Listening to the sound of rain on the roof is very peaceful and helps us relax our tired mind. Spending time with the people we love the most is the best part of the whole week. We must keep these sweet memories safe in our heart as the long years go by. When the bright sun goes down at night the sky turns beautiful shades of dark pink and purple. The tiny stars begin to shine bright like little diamonds in the high dark night sky.The round moon gives soft light to the earth and shows us the path in the dark woods. The busy town becomes very still and quiet as all the people go to their cozy homes. They get into their warm beds close their eyes and have very nice sweet dreams. A brand new day will come tomorrow with more fun adventures for everyone in the world. We can look to the future with a lot of hope love and happiness in our own life.
